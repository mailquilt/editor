A Social Block's icons that wrap onto a second line, as on a phone, have the same gap between the lines as between the icons. Before, the lines touched. Half the gap is above and below each icon, and the Block's padding above and below is that much less, so a single line of icons is as tall as before, in the email and on the canvas.
