Breaking: the Preview dialog is gone, and the Check Mode takes its place. Check shows the exported email in an inbox, at the email's width and in a phone's frame at 375px, with a line for it as an inbox lists it: the host's `sender` and `subject`, then the preheader, or the email's first words without one. Beside it, "Before you export" lists the email's Problems, from `checkEmail`, each with what's wrong and why it matters, and then what passed. Each Problem but an email too large for Gmail has a fix button, which opens Edit with that part selected and focus in the field that fixes it. With no unsubscribe link there's no field, so its button selects the last Row, where an email's footer is. With a Merge Tag that has a `sample`, "Show sample values" fills the tags with their samples. After a check, the Check tab shows how many Problems there are, and the count is announced. Problems never stop an export.

What changes for a host:

- The Preview dialog, and the Escape that closed it, are gone. A test of your own that clicked "Preview" now chooses the Check tab. Check's email is in sandboxed iframes titled "The email on a desktop" and "The email on a phone".
- The floating toolbar's button below the email is "Check the email", with a tick, and opens Check.
- The narrow notice below 640px has no button, as it no longer offers a preview. It's now a region named by its title, and takes focus when nothing else in the editor can.
- MJML loads the first time someone opens Check or exports. Check's email still needs `'unsafe-inline'` in `style-src`, as the Preview did.
- Theme tokens: `--mq-color-backdrop` is removed. `--mq-color-warning` (`#c2410c`, dark `#ff9b5e`), for the Problems' icons and the Check tab's count, `--mq-color-on-warning` (`#ffffff`, dark `#101113`), for the count's number, and `--mq-color-success` (`#15803d`, dark `#4ade80`), for the ticks of what passed and the "Ready to export" mark, are added.
- New properties on `<mail-quilt>` and `<mail-quilt-shell>`, and props on `MailQuilt` and `MailQuiltShell`: `sender` and `subject`, for Check's inbox. A Merge Tag in `mergeTags` can have a `sample`, which Check shows in its place, and `unsubscribe: true`, which marks the unsubscribe link Check looks for. The Shell and both React components pass them to the editor.
- `STRINGS`, `StringId` and `MailQuiltStrings` lose `preview.title`, `preview.close`, `preview.width`, `preview.desktop`, `preview.phone`, `preview.frame`, `preview.loading`, `preview.failed`, `canvas.preview` and `canvas.narrowPreview`. They gain `canvas.check`; `check.samples`, `check.desktop`, `check.phone`, `check.desktopFrame`, `check.phoneFrame`, `check.inbox`, `check.loading`, `check.failed`, `check.title`, `check.checking`, `check.toFixOne`, `check.toFixMany`, `check.allPassed`, `check.cantCheck`, `check.ready`, `check.readyText`, `check.toFix`, `check.passed`, `check.inEmail`, `check.inRow`, `check.inColumn`, `check.fixSaid` and `check.tabCount`; `passed.imageAlt`, `passed.links`, `passed.imageSrc`, `passed.preheader`, `passed.mergeTags`, `passed.size`, `passed.language`, `passed.contrast`, `passed.linkText` and `passed.unsubscribe`; and `problem.imageSrc`, `problem.imageSrcWhy`, `problem.imageSrcFix`, `problem.imageAlt`, `problem.imageAltWhy`, `problem.imageAltFix`, `problem.buttonLink`, `problem.buttonLinkWhy`, `problem.buttonLinkFix`, `problem.mergeTag`, `problem.mergeTagWhy`, `problem.mergeTagFix`, `problem.preheader`, `problem.preheaderWhy`, `problem.preheaderFix`, `problem.size`, `problem.sizeWhy`, `problem.language`, `problem.languageWhy`, `problem.languageFix`, `problem.textContrast`, `problem.linkContrast`, `problem.buttonContrast`, `problem.contrastWhy`, `problem.contrastFix`, `problem.linkText`, `problem.linkTextWhy`, `problem.linkTextFix`, `problem.unsubscribe`, `problem.unsubscribeWhy` and `problem.unsubscribeFix`. `canvas.narrowText` no longer says the email can be previewed. A translation written as an object typed `MailQuiltStrings` or `Record<StringId, string>` that sets a removed ID stops compiling, and a full one typed `Record<StringId, string>` also needs the new IDs. Once the editor runs, a removed ID is ignored.
- The renderer's `checkEmail` takes an `html` option, the email's HTML if it's rendered already, which it measures instead of rendering the email again. A contrast Problem has `contrast`, with the `color` and `background` as `#rrggbb`, the `ratio` and the `required` ratio, and a `vague-link-text` Problem has `linkText`, what the link says. The new `Contrast` type describes the first.
