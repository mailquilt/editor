The highlighted tag in the Merge Tag list, and a Merge Tag in text, have an edge at 3:1 or more, as a pressed button does. Before, the edge was at about 2:1, so a person with low vision couldn't easily tell which tag <kbd>Enter</kbd> would put in, or a tag from typed words. It uses `--mq-color-accent-strong`.
