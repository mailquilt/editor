Ctrl/⌘+K now reaches the host page where it does nothing in the editor, such as on a Button Block, or on a Row's toolbar or an empty Column with no Block that has text selected. Alt+← and Alt+→ no longer go back or forward a page from an empty Column's buttons, the side panel, its tabs and fields, the link field, the Preview and the email in it, or the notice shown when there's no room to edit, as they already didn't on a Block, in its text or in a toolbar.
