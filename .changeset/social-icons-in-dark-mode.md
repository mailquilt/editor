The Social Block's color icons can be seen in dark mode. X, Threads and TikTok are white on a black rounded square, and the website icon white on dark grey. Drawn black on their own, they disappeared where an email app's dark mode turned a white background near-black, as Outlook, Outlook.com, Yahoo and the Gmail app on iPhone do. The Side panel says that white icons need their dark background, which some email apps' dark modes make light, and that color icons show on any background.
