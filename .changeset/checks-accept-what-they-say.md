A link or image URL that starts with `httpſ:` in place of `https:`, or a color with anything but a plain space after a comma, such as `rgb(0,\n0,0)` or a no-break space, is refused when set. Neither is a link or a color the docs describe. The same goes for a new language code with `ſ` or the Kelvin sign `K` in place of s or k. A saved language still loads whatever it is.

An email saved with 0.1.0 that holds one of these still opens. Emails are now saved as version 2 of the format, and a version 1 email is upgraded as it loads: such a color gets a plain space after each comma, and such a URL starts with `https:`. Mail Quilt 0.1.0 refuses a version 2 email as newer, so update every package that reads your emails, such as a renderer on your server, at the same time.
