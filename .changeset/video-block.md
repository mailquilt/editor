The Video Block, part of format version 4: a picture that links to a video, since email can't play one. It has the video's `url`, a `thumbnail` and `alt`, checked as an Image's are, and `play`, how the email shows it's a video: `"below"`, a line under the picture, a ▶ in a circle and `label` ("Watch the video" unless it says otherwise), in `color` or the Email style's links' color; `"on"`, a play button on the picture, which is `playThumbnail`, a copy of the thumbnail with one drawn on it; or `"none"`. A Video without both a link and a thumbnail is left out, as an Image without a `src` is, and one set to `"on"` without a drawn copy shows the line. A new `thumbnail` without a new `playThumbnail` removes the old copy and turns `"on"` back to `"below"`, in `updateBlockProps` itself.

The renderer writes the picture as MJML's image, linked to the video, with the line in a row of its own under it. `renderText` writes a Video as its alt text and its line's words and link. `checkEmail` reports a Video without a link as `video-without-link` on `url`, one without a thumbnail as `image-without-src` on `thumbnail`, and one without alt text as `image-without-alt`. Its line's contrast is `low-link-contrast` on `color` and its words `vague-link-text` on `label`, and its link counts as an unsubscribe link once it shows. Only the parts of a Video the email shows are checked for unknown Merge Tags.

In the editor, a Video's Selection bar has its link, a Thumbnail popover with Upload image, its alt text, and its Play button, whose three choices each say what they mean. A YouTube link fills in YouTube's own thumbnail for the video. "On the picture" draws a play button on a copy of the thumbnail in the browser and uploads it through `uploadImage`, while the canvas says "Adding the play button…". Without `uploadImage` it's off and says why, and where the thumbnail's site doesn't allow CORS, the editor says so and offers to upload the thumbnail. A new thumbnail is drawn on again. Email style's links' color, text color, body size and body font reach a Video's line. Check's "Every Image has an image", "Every Image has alt text or is decorative" and "Every Button and link goes somewhere" now count Videos too.

Breaking: `BlockType` and `BlockProps` gain `video`, and `ProblemCode` gains `video-without-link`, so a `switch` that handles every type or code no longer compiles until it handles them (measured with `tsc`). `STRINGS`, `StringId` and `MailQuiltStrings` gain `block.video`, `video.empty`, `video.emptyWhy`, `video.noThumbnail`, `video.addingPlay`, `bar.videoUrl`, `bar.thumbnail`, `bar.addThumbnail`, `bar.thumbnailWaits`, `bar.describeThumbnail`, `bar.playButton`, `bar.uploadThumbnail`, `field.lineColor`, `field.play`, `field.thumbnail`, `field.thumbnail.description`, `field.videoAlt.description`, `field.videoLabel`, `field.videoUrl`, `field.videoUrl.description`, `play.below`, `play.below.description`, `play.on`, `play.on.description`, `play.on.noUploads`, `play.none`, `play.none.description`, `play.failed`, `play.cantDraw`, `play.cantDrawThis`, `play.added`, `invalid.play`, `invalid.videoLabel`, `problem.videoThumbnail`, `problem.videoThumbnailWhy`, `problem.videoThumbnailFix`, `problem.videoAlt`, `problem.videoAltWhy`, `problem.videoLink`, `problem.videoLinkWhy` and `problem.videoLinkFix`, and the English of `passed.imageAlt`, `passed.links` and `passed.imageSrc` changes. As for the Menu Block, a partial translation typed `MailQuiltStrings` and one spread from `STRINGS` still compile; a full one typed `Record<StringId, string>` and written out ID by ID needs the new IDs.
