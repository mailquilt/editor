The [quick start](./quick-start) gets the editor working. This page is about the page around it: where it goes and how much room it needs. To match its colors and font to your app, see [Theme the editor](./theme-the-editor). If the editor is a page of its own, start with the [Shell](./shell), which does most of this for you.

## Shell or your own page

Use the Shell when the editor is the whole page, and its top bar can hold the page's controls. It fills the space you give it, scrolls the email inside itself and shows every message.

Put `<mail-quilt>` in a page of your own when the editor sits among other things, such as below a form for the email's subject and recipients, or when you want your own buttons around it. The rest of this page is for that case. The [Shell page](./shell#shell-or-mail-quilt) compares the two.

## Width

`<mail-quilt>` takes the width of the space you give it. Inside, the email sits on a workspace, with the Selection bar across its top.

- **The Rows list is before the email.** It shows each Row as a small picture, 232px wide, where the editor is at least the email's width plus 632px. The email is {{email-width}}px wide unless someone changes it in the Selection bar, so that's 1232px, which leaves room for More to dock too. Narrower, it's collapsed to the Rows' numbers, 52px wide. Once someone expands or collapses it, it stays that way, and expanded where it doesn't fit beside the email, it opens over the workspace's start.
- **The Add panel, of Blocks and empty Rows, docks before the email** in the Rows list's place while it's open and the editor is at least the email's width plus 368px, 968px for the usual email. Any narrower, and the panel opens over the workspace's start rather than squeeze the email.
- **More, the Selection bar's panel for the rest of a Block's settings, docks beside the email** while the editor is at least the email's width plus 400px and the Rows list's width: 1232px for the usual email with the list expanded, 1052px with it collapsed, or 1288px with the Add panel docked. Otherwise it opens over the workspace.
- **In Style, the Email style panel, 320px wide, docks beside the email** while the editor is at least the email's width plus 400px, 1000px for the usual email. Otherwise it opens over the workspace's end, and the email stays its width.
- **In Check, the email is shown at its own width and at a phone's 375px, beside a 352px panel of Problems.** Where they don't quite fit side by side, both are drawn up to a fifth smaller to stay so. With less room, the phone goes below, and where the desktop inbox doesn't fit either, both are drawn smaller together.
- **The Selection bar keeps to one line.** Where it's short of room for what's selected, its Move, Duplicate and Delete buttons move into More.
- **Below {{editor-min-width}}px, there's no room to edit.** The editor shows a notice in its place, whichever Mode it's in. It comes back, with the same email, once there's room again.

So give the editor the full width of your content area. Don't put it in a narrow column beside a sidebar of your own.

## Height

The editor is at least {{editor-min-height}}px tall, and grows with the email. It fills any height you give it, but never shrinks below the email, so the page scrolls rather than the editor. As it does, the top bar, with Undo, Redo and the Modes, stays in view at the top of the window, the Selection bar, the Rows list and the Add panel below it, in Edit, the button that opens Check at the bottom, and in Check, its panel of Problems.

That means:

- **Let the page scroll.** Put `<mail-quilt>` in the page's normal flow, with no fixed height, and nothing that clips it.
- **For a box of a fixed height**, such as a panel in a dashboard, use the Shell instead. It scrolls the email inside itself and keeps the Add panel in view.

Here's the editor below a form of the app's own:

```html title="index.html"
<main class="compose">
  <form class="compose-fields">
    <label>Subject <input name="subject" /></label>
    <label
      >Send to
      <select name="list">
        …
      </select></label
    >
  </form>
  <mail-quilt></mail-quilt>
</main>
```

```css title="styles.css"
.compose {

}
```

## Check it before you ship

- **Narrow the window** below {{editor-min-width}}px and back. The notice should show, then the editor with the same email.
- **Use it from the keyboard.** Tab into the editor, select a Block, move it with Alt and the arrow keys, and edit its text. The [keyboard page](./keyboard) lists every key.
- **With your own colors, check contrast**: text against the surface and workspace, the selected Block's tag, focus rings and the tooltip.
- **Open Check**, and export the HTML once. If your page has a Content Security Policy, [Content Security Policy](./api#content-security-policy) says what Check needs.
