**The editor partly meets WCAG 2.2 at level AA.** It meets every criterion that applies, except one: below {{editor-min-width}} pixels wide, such as at 400% zoom, it can't be edited at all.

This page covers `<mail-quilt>` and `<mail-quilt-shell>`, version 0.2. It makes no claim about the emails people design with them. Those are their authors' content. To make an email accessible, give every Image alt text that says what it shows, and give each Button words that make sense on their own.

## What doesn't meet AA

**1.4.10 Reflow.** When the editor is narrower than {{editor-min-width}} pixels, as at 400% zoom on a 1280-pixel screen, it shows a notice in place of the canvas and the side panel. The email can still be previewed, but not changed.

WCAG allows content that needs a two-dimensional layout to scroll in both directions, and an email that is {{email-width}} pixels wide is that kind of content. The side panel isn't, so its settings should still be reachable at that width. They aren't yet.

At 200% zoom on a 1280-pixel screen, an editor that has the page's full width has 640 pixels, and everything works. The side panel moves below the email. An editor that shares the page, with a sidebar beside it for example, is narrower than that and shows the notice.

## How it was checked

The audit was done on 19 November 2026.

- **Automated checks.** [axe-core](https://github.com/dequelabs/axe-core) 4.14, with its WCAG 2.2 AA rules, ran against the Shell and the editor in Chromium 158, Firefox 159 and WebKit 27.2. It checked nothing selected, a Block selected, each tab of the side panel, the link field showing an error, and Preview open. It found no violations.
- **The keyboard.** The editor's browser tests press every key on the [Keyboard and screen readers](./keyboard) page in the same three browsers.
- **Measurements in the browser.** These covered the size of every control, the contrast of control edges and states, extra text spacing, widths from 320 to 1280 pixels, and forced colors as the browser emulates them.
- **Reading the code.** Every criterion below was checked against the source, including what each control is named and what a screen reader is told.

Screen readers, real browser zoom and Windows High Contrast haven't been checked by hand yet.

## Known limits outside WCAG

- **Safari and the Preview.** In Safari, <kbd>Escape</kbd> doesn't close the Preview once focus is inside the email. <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>Tab</kbd> goes back to its Close button.
- **The host page.** The page title, landmarks and a way to skip to the editor belong to the page the editor is on.

## Report a problem

If something in the editor is hard to use with a screen reader, the keyboard, zoom or another tool, please [open an issue](https://github.com/mailquilt/editor/issues). Say which browser and tool you used.
