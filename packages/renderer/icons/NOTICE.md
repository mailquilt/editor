# The Social Block's icons

These PNGs are the icons of the Social Block. Sent emails load them from jsDelivr, at the version of `@mailquilt/renderer` that rendered the email, unless the Host app gives `socialIconsUrl` and serves this folder itself. Keep this file with them.

## Trademarks

The logos are trademarks of their owners. Mail Quilt isn't affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any of them. The person sending an email follows each brand's own guidelines for its logo.

When a brand's owner asks, its icon is removed from the next release. That changes the emails rendered after it. Emails already sent keep loading the icon from the version they name.

## Sources

Each icon is the brand's own mark, recolored and drawn as a 96px PNG by `scripts/social-icons.ts`. The color icons add white behind the parts each brand draws in white, such as Facebook's "f". In color, X, Threads and TikTok are white on a black rounded square, and the website icon white on dark grey, so they can be seen in dark mode.
